# AeroHome Manufacturing – Operational Performance Dashboard

## Business Problem

AeroHome Manufacturing is experiencing inconsistent manufacturing performance across its plants. Although the company collects data on planned production, actual production, defective units, quality inspections, machine downtime, and energy consumption, management lacks a centralized analytical solution to identify why production targets are missed and where operational inefficiencies and financial exposure are occurring.

The key business problem is:

> **How can AeroHome improve manufacturing performance by identifying production gaps, quality issues, machine downtime, and energy inefficiencies across its plants?**

The analysis focuses on production performance, production shortfalls, quality defects, machine downtime, energy consumption, and supplier delivery risks.

### Business Objective

Identify the key factors affecting manufacturing performance and provide data-driven insights that help management improve production efficiency, product quality, machine utilization, and operational performance.

### Business Questions

1. How effectively are manufacturing plants achieving planned production targets?
2. Which products contribute most to production shortfalls?
3. Which machines experience the highest downtime?
4. What are the major defect types affecting product quality?
5. Which plants have higher energy consumption per unit?
6. Which suppliers present higher delivery risks?
7. Where should management focus to improve manufacturing performance?

## KPIs / Features Explained

The dashboard contains five primary KPI cards.

### 1. Production Achievement %

Measures the percentage of planned production achieved.

**Business Purpose:** Evaluates how effectively manufacturing plants meet production targets.

### 2. Production Shortfall

Measures the gap between planned production and actual production.

**Business Purpose:** Identifies production gaps requiring management attention.

### 3. Defect Rate %

Measures the proportion of defective units relative to the relevant production quantity.

**Business Purpose:** Helps management monitor product quality.

### 4. Total Downtime Hours

Measures the total time machines remain unavailable due to downtime.

**Business Purpose:** Helps identify machine availability issues and operational interruptions.

### 5. Total Procurement Spend

Measures the total procurement expenditure recorded in the available data.

**Business Purpose:** Helps management monitor procurement spending.

### Dashboard Filters

The dashboard provides three interactive slicers:

- Plant
- Product
- Date

These filters allow management to explore manufacturing performance from different business perspectives.

### 4. Defects by Defect Type

Compares defective units across different defect categories.

**Business Use:**
- Identify major defect types.
- Monitor quality issues.

Compares energy consumption per unit across manufacturing plants.

**Business Use:**
- Identify plants with higher energy consumption per unit.
- Compare energy efficiency.
- Highlight opportunities to investigate energy usage.

### 6. Supplier Delivery Risk

Highlights supplier delivery risk based on the available supplier information.

**Business Use:**
- Identify suppliers with potential delivery risks.
- Support supplier performance monitoring.
- Help management investigate supply-related concerns.

## Recommendations / Conclusion

### Recommendations

Based on the analysis, AeroHome management can:

- Monitor production achievement regularly.
- Investigate plants and products with higher production shortfalls.
- Prioritize maintenance based on machine downtime patterns.
- Strengthen quality monitoring for major defect types.
- Investigate plants with higher energy consumption per unit.
- Monitor supplier delivery risks.
- Use dashboard filters to investigate plant-, product-, and date-level performance.

### Conclusion

The AeroHome Manufacturing analysis provides a consolidated view of manufacturing performance across production, quality, machine downtime, energy consumption, and supplier delivery risk.

The dashboard helps management identify production gaps, operational inefficiencies, and areas requiring further investigation.

The project supports a **data-driven manufacturing improvement approach** by converting operational data into meaningful insights for management decision-making.
